Apex Electronics Ltd — FY2023 Board Paper — Appendix B

Section B1. NZ Leadership Update
Following an extensive search process, the Board confirms the permanent appointment of Mr. James O'Connor as General Manager, Apex Electronics NZ, effective 1 January 2023. Mr. O'Connor joins from a senior operating role at a regional electronics distributor and brings 14 years of channel and supply-chain experience to the role.
The Board considers this appointment closes the leadership transition initiated in mid-2022 and provides a stable platform for execution of the FY2023 NZ growth plan. No further changes to the NZ executive team are anticipated in the FY2023 horizon.

Section B2. Customer Concentration — NZ Channel
Approximately one third of NZ FY2022 channel revenue was delivered through a single reseller relationship (Security Tech Inc.). Diversification of the NZ customer base is a stated FY2023 priority of the new GM.
